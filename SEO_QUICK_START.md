## 🆘 Common Questions

**Q: How long until I see results?**
A: Basic visibility: 2-4 weeks. Meaningful traffic: 3-6 months. Top rankings: 6-12 months.

**Q: What's the #1 most important thing?**
A: Google Business Profile + Reviews. This is 70% of local SEO success.

**Q: How often should I post on Instagram?**
A: Minimum 3x per week. Optimal: 5-7x per week with stories daily.

**Q: Do I need to blog?**
A: Not required but highly recommended. 1-2 quality posts per month can double organic traffic.

**Q: Should I pay for ads?**
A: Optional. Google Ads can complement SEO but isn't required. Focus on organic first.

**Q: What if I get a negative review?**
A: Respond professionally and promptly. Offer to resolve the issue. Shows you care about clients.
