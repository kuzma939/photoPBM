Create and optimize your Google Business Profile:
1. Go to google.com/business
2. Add your photography business
3. Select "Photographer" as business type
4. Add Barcelona as your location
5. Upload high-quality photos
6. Get client reviews (crucial for local SEO!)
7. Post regular updates

### 2. Image Optimization
For all photos on your website:
```
Good: alt="Romantic couple photoshoot at Gothic Quarter Barcelona"
Bad: alt="IMG_1234.jpg"
```

**Best practices:**
- Use descriptive alt text with location keywords
- Compress images (use .webp or .avif format - you're already using .avif ✅)
- Include location in file names: `gothic-quarter-love-story-1.avif`
